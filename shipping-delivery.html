Shipping & Delivery Policy

Pure, chemical-free organic essentials harvested in small batches and delivered farm-fresh to your doorstep with guaranteed packaging integrity.

Bangalore Express Delivery

Orders delivered within 24 to 48 hours across Bangalore urban and suburban pin codes directly from our local dispatch hub in Gunjur.

Free Shipping Threshold

Enjoy 100% FREE Delivery on all orders of ₹999 and above. For orders below ₹999, a flat nominal delivery fee of ₹40 applies.

Pan-India Express Shipping

Shipments outside Bangalore are fulfilled via tier-1 air & express courier partners (Delhivery, BlueDart, India Post) reaching within 3 to 5 business days.

Eco-Insulated Breakage Proof

Glass honey and pickle containers are encased in custom molded shock-absorbent eco-packaging to guarantee zero leakages or transit damage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order status?
Once your order is confirmed, you will receive real-time SMS & email dispatch notifications containing your AWB / tracking number. You can also view live status in the My Orders section of your profile.
What if my items are damaged during transit?
We take extreme pride in our packaging. In the rare event of a transit mishap or broken seal, notify our customer care team within 24 hours with a photograph and we will arrange an instant free replacement or full refund.
Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
If your order has not yet been dispatched, contact our manager Siddaiah immediately at +91 6302017482 to update your shipping address.
